🐍 WHAT IS YOUR SNAKE? (THE GIANT GREEN ANACONDA)

You control a giant, realistic Green Anaconda (Eunectes murinus)—the heaviest snake on Earth, famous for its olive-green scales, dark oval blotches, muscular body, and flicking forked tongue. Instead of biting with poison alone, a real anaconda wraps around its target and squeezes (constricts) until it crushes it, then swallows food whole (leaving a visible lump moving down its stomach).
